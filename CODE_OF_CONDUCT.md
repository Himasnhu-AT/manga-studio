# Code of conduct

Manga Studio follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good faith, critique work and not people, and keep harassment,
personal attacks and explicit content out of issues, discussions, PRs and shared images.

Report problems to the maintainer at hyattherate2005@gmail.com. Reports are kept private.
The maintainer may remove content and block anyone who breaks these rules.
